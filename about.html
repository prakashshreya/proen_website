What Defines Us

PROEN Consulting Services Pvt. Ltd is a subsidiary of Carvewing Solutions and an exclusive platform-agnostic CLM service provider. We bring cutting-edge, end-to-end Contract Lifecycle Management expertise to organizations across industries, helping them track, manage, and automate every granular step of the contract lifecycle across both buy-side and sell-side contracting — so contract management becomes simple, not a bottleneck.
