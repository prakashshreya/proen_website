FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Any Questions?

Answers to what organizations most often ask before, during, and after engaging PROEN Consulting Services for Contract Lifecycle Management implementation, managed services, staff augmentation, and legacy data extraction.
Get More Help
  • What is Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM)?
    CLM is the process of managing a contract from initial request through drafting, negotiation, execution, and ongoing obligations, milestones, renewals, and compliance. PROEN provides cutting-edge, end-to-end CLM expertise that helps organizations track, manage, and automate every granular step of that lifecycle.
  • Do you support both buy-side and sell-side contracts?
    Yes. Our CLM expertise spans both buy-side contracting (procurement and vendor agreements) and sell-side contracting (sales and customer agreements), so a single engagement can standardize processes across your organization's full contract portfolio.
  • Is PROEN platform-agnostic — can you work with our existing CLM tool?
    Yes. PROEN is an exclusive platform-agnostic CLM service provider. Our Technology Development and integration expertise let us work with the CLM platform you already run, or guide you through selecting and configuring a new one, rather than locking you into a single vendor.
  • What does your Legacy Data Extraction service involve, and how accurate is it?
    Our Legacy Data Extractor converts paper-based and legacy contracts into structured digital data — with custom fields, a unified cloud repository, and intelligent search — at a data extraction accuracy of over 98%, human-verified.
  • How does staff augmentation pricing work?
    Our Staff Augmentation service provides skilled, on-demand professionals — functional consultants, program managers, technologists, and paralegals — who scale your existing team flexibly and cost-effectively. Scope, duration, and pricing are tailored per engagement; get in touch and we'll put together a quote for your specific needs.
  • Are you ISO certified?
    Yes. PROEN Consulting Services is ISO certified, reflecting our commitment to consistent, auditable quality management across every CLM engagement we deliver.
  • Do you have an office in the USA?
    Yes. Alongside our India headquarters in Bengaluru, PROEN maintains a USA office at 1151 Walker Road, Suite 535, Dover, Delaware 19904.
  • What is Contract Diligence?
    Contract Diligence is PROEN's ChatGPT-integrated plug-in for proficient contract and document authoring — covering service agreements, NDAs, and more. It takes you from simple setup and template/clause selection through review, generation, and cloud-storage integration, with everything kept encrypted and confidential.
PROEN Consulting Services delivers platform-agnostic Contract Lifecycle Management — from digital transformation and implementation consulting to managed services, staff augmentation and legacy data extraction.
