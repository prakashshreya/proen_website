Contract Diligence

Custom Enterprise Pricing

Contract Diligence is PROEN's ChatGPT-integrated plug-in for proficient contract and document authoring. It helps legal and commercial teams draft service agreements, NDAs, and other core contract types quickly, consistently, and securely — with encrypted, confidential handling throughout.

Request A Demo

  • How It Works
  • Client Feedback (2)

Contract Diligence brings ChatGPT-integrated authoring directly into PROEN's contract lifecycle workflow, so legal and business teams can produce accurate, on-brand contracts and documents without waiting on ad-hoc drafting cycles. It is built to handle common instrument types — service agreements, NDAs, and other core contract templates — while keeping every document encrypted and confidential from first draft to final signature.

  1. Simple Setup — Connect Contract Diligence to your workspace in minutes, with no complex configuration required.
  2. Fill Basic Information — Enter the core parties, dates, and commercial details for the document you need.
  3. Customization — Select from PROEN's clause and template library to tailor the document to your requirements.
  4. Review & Edit — Use the ChatGPT-integrated assistant to refine language, check consistency, and resolve open issues.
  5. Generate & Share — Produce a polished, ready-to-send contract or document in a fraction of the usual time.
  6. Integration — Save and sync the finished document with your existing cloud storage and CLM repository.
