Digital Transformation

Comprehensive digital transformation solutions tailored to optimize your contract lifecycle management processes.

Consultation on CLM Implementation

Specialized guidance for integrating and maximizing the benefits of your CLM solution, from needs assessment to deployment.

Paralegal Service

Expert legal support — research, document preparation, legacy data abstraction, template and clause creation, redlining and negotiation support.

Technology Development

Platform-agnostic technology solutions with AI/ML-enabled data abstraction, analytics and seamless integration with your existing systems.

Managed Services

End-to-end support — contract creation, repository management, monitoring, renewals, compliance and auditing, so nothing falls through the cracks.

Staff Augmentation

Skilled on-demand professionals across functional consulting, program management, technology and paralegal roles to scale your team flexibly.

Data Extraction as a Service

Our Legacy Data Extractor converts paper-based and legacy contracts into structured digital data, with better than 98% human-verified accuracy.
